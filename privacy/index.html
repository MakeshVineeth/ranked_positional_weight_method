Privacy Policy for Calculate RPW

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 | Applies to Android, Web, and Desktop

Overview

Calculate RPW ("the App") is an offline assembly line balancing calculator developed by MakeshTech. Your privacy matters, and the App is designed to run entirely on your device without collecting or transmitting personal data.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, or account credentials)
  • Assembly line inputs, calculations, or generated reports
  • Location data, device identifiers, or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, cookies for tracking, or crash diagnostics

Local Storage and Device Permissions

The Android release version of Calculate RPW requests zero special Android permissions and operates without internet access.

Only your selected visual theme preference (System, Light, or Dark) is saved locally on your device using standard platform storage (SharedPreferences on Android/Windows or browser localStorage on the web). You can clear this preference at any time by clearing app data or site storage.

Clipboard access only occurs when you explicitly tap a result card or select Copy Report.

Third-Party Services

Calculate RPW contains no advertisements, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no external API calls. When accessed via GitHub Pages, standard static web hosting access logs are managed by GitHub under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or any other age group.

Changes and Contact

Updates to this policy will appear on this page and in the project repository. Questions or feedback can be submitted through the GitHub issue tracker.
