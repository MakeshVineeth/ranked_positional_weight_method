# Privacy Policy

Calculate RPW runs entirely offline on your device. No personal information, assembly line inputs, or telemetry ever leave your device.

## Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any personal data, location data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports.

## Local Preferences and Permissions

- **Android Permissions**: The Android release build declares zero special permissions and does not request internet access.
- **On-Device Storage**: Only your selected theme mode (System, Light, or Dark) is saved locally via standard platform preferences.
- **Clipboard**: The app writes to your clipboard only when you tap a result card or press **Copy Report**.
